# Email draft: request access to the FDMSE-ISL dataset

**To:** soumitra.samanta(at)gm.rkmvu.ac.in (replace "(at)" with "@")
**Subject:** Request for research access to the FDMSE-ISL dataset (SignBridge, Indian Sign Language for healthcare)

---

Dear Dr. Samanta,

I am Anirudh Raj K, and I am building **SignBridge**, an Indian Sign Language communication aid
that helps deaf patients and doctors talk during clinic visits. A patient signs to a phone
camera and the doctor reads it as English. The doctor's reply is shown back as text and signed
by an avatar. Recognition runs on the patient's device, so no video leaves the phone.

I read your paper, *Hierarchical Windowed Graph Attention Network and a Large Scale Dataset
for Isolated Indian Sign Language Recognition* (arXiv:2407.14224). FDMSE-ISL, with 2,002 words
signed by 20 deaf signers, is exactly the kind of data SignBridge needs. Today the app is
trained on INCLUDE (263 words), which leaves out many words patients need in hospital.

I would like to ask whether the FDMSE-ISL videos or pose keypoints could be shared with me,
and under what terms. Specifically:

1. **Use:** training an isolated-sign recognition model for SignBridge, and evaluating it on
   held-out signers.
2. **Form:** pose or hand keypoints alone would be enough; we do not need to redistribute videos.
3. **Credit:** your paper and dataset would be cited in the app and its documentation.
4. **Licence:** please let me know whether use in a free, non-commercial healthcare app is
   allowed, and what would be needed if the app is later offered more widely.

I am happy to sign a data use agreement and to share our results, and any improvements to
medical vocabulary, with your group.

Thank you for your work for the Deaf community.
