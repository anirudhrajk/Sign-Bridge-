## 1. ISLRTC (Indian Sign Language dictionary)

**To:** islrtcnewdelhi@gmail.com
**Subject:** Permission to use ISL dictionary medical signs in a healthcare app for deaf patients

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am Anirudh Raj K, and I am building SignBridge, an app that helps deaf patients and doctors
talk during clinic visits. A patient signs to a phone camera and the doctor reads English. The
doctor's reply is signed back by an on-screen signer. Video stays on the patient's device.

Today SignBridge knows very few medical signs. I would like to ask for permission to use the
medical signs from the ISLRTC Indian Sign Language dictionary. Specifically:

1. **Use:** teaching the app to show these signs through its on-screen signer, and to recognise them.
2. **Form:** we would store body and hand positions taken from the videos, not the videos themselves.
3. **Credit:** ISLRTC would be credited in the app and its documentation.
4. **Terms:** please tell me what is allowed for a free healthcare app, and what would be needed
   if it is later offered more widely.

If ISLRTC can also suggest deaf signers who could review our signs, that would help us check
they are correct before patients rely on them.

Thank you for your work for the Deaf community.
