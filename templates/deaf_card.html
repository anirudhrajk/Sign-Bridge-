I am Deaf.

I use {{ languages.get(lang, 'sign language') }}. My name is {{ current_user.display_name }}.

  1. Please don't call my name. I can't hear it. Tap my shoulder, wave, or call me with SignBridge.
  2. Face me when you talk, in good light. Don't cover your mouth.
  3. Use SignBridge or write it down. Short, simple sentences are best.
  4. Please book a sign language interpreter for important talks: diagnosis, surgery, consent, and going home.
  5. Check that I understood. Ask me to show or tell you back. Nodding does not always mean yes.
  6. In an emergency, show me, point, and use the emergency screen.
