Made for the exam room, not the lab.

Deaf patients often leave appointments unsure what was said. Every feature here exists to close that gap.

Sign to text

The camera follows the patient's hands on the device and shows the doctor clear English text.

Speech to sign

The doctor speaks naturally. The patient sees the words and the matching signs on screen.

Emergency mode

Large, one-tap phrases like "I can't breathe" reach staff instantly, even under stress.

Patient confirms first

Nothing is sent until the patient checks it. Translation mistakes never reach the chart silently.

Works on weak networks

Hand tracking runs on the device, so clinics with poor internet can still use it.

Private by design

Video never leaves the device. Consent is recorded, and patients can delete their history.

How it works

One conversation, two languages.

The patient and doctor each use the mode that feels natural to them.

  1. The patient signs to the cameraHand movement is tracked on the device and translated into English.
  2. The patient checks the textThey can fix any word before it is sent, so the doctor reads what was meant.
  3. The doctor replies by voiceTheir words appear on the patient's screen, with the matching signs.
