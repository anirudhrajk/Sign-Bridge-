    Questions worth asking

    What does SignBridge do?

    A deaf patient signs to the camera and the doctor reads it as text. The doctor speaks or types, and an avatar signs the reply with each word captioned.

    Which sign languages does it know?

    Indian Sign Language with 262 signs and American Sign Language with 250 signs. Patients choose their language in Settings.

    Does my video leave the device?

    No. Tracking and sign recognition run on the phone or laptop.

    Does it work without internet?

    Face-to-face mode needs no internet and no server. The app also installs on phones like a native app.

    What are its limits?

    It reads one sign at a time and knows a few hundred everyday words, few of them medical. The avatar signs word by word in English order, not full sign language grammar. The patient always checks a message before it is sent.

    Can it replace an interpreter?

    No. SignBridge is a communication aid. For consent, diagnosis and discharge, book a qualified interpreter.
